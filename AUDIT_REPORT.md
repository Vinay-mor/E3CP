# E3CP simulation audit report

Specific code-path audit of QoS-aware node wake-up and final-interval consolidation accounting. This report does not claim that the overall analytical model is validated against hardware.

## Fixes applied

1. **QoS-aware node wake-up.** Previously, a capacity-feasible awake node could cause E3CP to use its QoS-relaxing fallback even when an asleep node satisfied both reliability and estimated-latency constraints. The wake-up decision now checks QoS-feasible sleepers and wakes a selected feasible candidate before fallback. The E3CP-no-consolidation ablation follows the same rule.
2. **Final-boundary consolidation.** The heuristic estimates benefit over one subsequent interval. Consolidation and migration cost are now skipped at the final boundary, where no future interval exists to realize the assumed saving.
3. **Metric timing alignment.** Powered-on-node and utilization samples are now recorded at the pre-boundary snapshot also used to compute interval node energy. A consolidation at the boundary changes the following interval, not the snapshot already measured.
4. **Fresh Figure 5.** The combined request-failure/active-node figure is generated by `run_experiments.py` directly from the latest aggregate CSV rather than relying on a stale image.

## Interpretation and limitations

E3CP improves modeled energy and estimated latency over First-Fit in this synthetic simulation, but it is not the best variant on every metric. Min-Energy + Consolidation uses less modeled energy than E3CP. Latency-Aware without consolidation has a lower estimated latency and request-failure rate than E3CP. Conclusions should describe this trade-off rather than claim universal superiority.

Energy and latency are analytical proxies; network/image-transfer/sleep-wake energy, migration duration, transient service disruption, and calibration against real hardware are not included. The generated traces and node fleet are synthetic. These fixes address the two audited code paths; they are not a comprehensive proof of scientific validity.
